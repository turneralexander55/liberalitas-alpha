LIBERALITAS
Alpha test

Thanks for helping me test this. Liberalitas is the app I've been building for keeping track of coins, bullion and silver. This page walks you through installing it, what I'd like you to try, and sending me your thoughts. Your answers save on your phone as you go, so you can stop and pick it back up later.

1 · Install

  1. On your phone, tap Get the Android app below. It downloads a file ending in .apk.
  2. Open the download (pull down your notifications, or look in Files, Downloads). If Android asks, let your browser install unknown apps, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown developer, tap More details, then Install anyway. It says that because the app isn't on the Play Store yet.
  4. Open Liberalitas from your app drawer.

Needs Android 8 or newer. Most updates show up on their own when you reopen the app. If I send you a new install link, install it over the old one and your stuff stays.

Heads up: the iPhone version is rough

Until I get a proper iPhone build set up, the app runs inside Expo Go, a free app for trying out apps that are still being built. It'll be a real iPhone app by the beta. For now:

  • You need internet the first time you open it. If it won't load, try again later and text me.
  • The first load is slow, and you might hit a reload or an error screen. If you get an error screen, screenshot it for me.
  • These don't work on iPhone yet: Face ID lock, automatic backups, moving to a new phone, tap to trade, and background price alerts. Just mark those Skip.
  • Your collection lives inside Expo Go, so deleting Expo Go deletes it.

Everything else (scanning, adding coins, the vault, reports, backups) is the real app, and that's where your notes help me most.

  1. Get Expo Go from the App Store. It's free.
  2. If you got an email invite to my Expo team, accept it, then sign in to Expo Go with that account.
  3. On your iPhone, tap Open in Expo Go below. It opens the app inside Expo Go.
  4. Give the first load up to a minute. Then Liberalitas starts up.
  5. After that, open Expo Go and tap Liberalitas under Recently opened.

Needs iOS 16 or newer. You get my latest version each time you open it.

2 · Before you start

Good to know

  • This is early. Things will break, and finding them is the whole point.
  • You get Pro for free. You won't be charged for anything. To see what the free version is like, turn off Test Pro on the Pro screen. It turns back on when you restart the app.
  • Scanning uses AI. The app walks you through getting a free Google key (Settings, AI for identification). It takes a couple of minutes and doesn't need a card.
  • Everything stays on your phone. There's no account, and I can't see your collection.

Don't worry about these

  • Buying scan credits or Pro. I haven't hooked up the app stores yet.
  • The Spanish setting still showing English.
  • Metal prices updating slowly.
  • Moving between an Android and an iPhone. That needs the stores too.
  • On iPhone, the things listed as not working yet.
  • Links to the app store pages. They aren't up yet.

One important thing

Uninstalling the app deletes everything in it. If you put your real collection in, keep your own record too, or make a backup (it's on the list below).
